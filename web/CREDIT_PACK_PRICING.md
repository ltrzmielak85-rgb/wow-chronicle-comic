# Proposed credit-pack pricing — confirmed, subject to cost testing

| Pack | Non-expiring credits | Proposed USD price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Small | 10 | $4.99 |
| Medium | 25 | $9.99 |
| Large | 50 | $17.99 |

Packs are available to subscribers without changing their subscription. Spend expiring monthly credits before purchased credits. One credit buys one generated comic page. Purchases must be verified by Stripe before granting credits. Refunds/chargebacks require reconciliation.

The catalog is intentionally inactive in SQL until measured AI generation cost, retry overhead, Stripe fees, taxes and margins are reviewed. This commit does not enable checkout or activate payment products.
