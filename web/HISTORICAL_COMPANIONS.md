# Historical companion tracking — confirmed

Track Hunter pets, Warlock demons and other recurring companions as historical observations. Use only observations at or before a quest's recorded completion time. An explicit 'none' or 'unknown' observation prevents silently reusing a previously active companion. Do not infer past pets from the player's current pet. Approved, versioned companion reference images guide visual consistency across panels.

Foundation: companion identity, time-stamped observations and versioned reference sheet tables with owner-read RLS; historical selection helper and draft tests. Actual WoW API capture, importer integration, companion reference generation/approval UI, trusted write validation and worker wiring remain outstanding. The addon may not be able to reliably identify every temporary summon; record uncertainty rather than fabricate facts.
