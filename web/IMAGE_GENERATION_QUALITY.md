# Launch image-generation quality — confirmed

All subscribers and free users receive the same consistent high-quality generation setting. There is no Premium image tier and no adaptive quality pricing at launch. Every successfully delivered comic page costs exactly one credit, regardless of layout, story importance or subscription plan. A failed comic releases the full reservation under the existing all-or-nothing policy.

The policy is provider-neutral: exact model, resolution, output format and prompt settings remain to be selected after quality and unit-cost benchmarks. High-quality is a product target, not proof of image output quality. Do not interpret the constant as a working image generation service.

Committed: typed launch policy, deterministic quote helper, and draft tests. Not implemented: model provider integration, actual generation worker, cost benchmarking, production retries and execution of tests.
