# Included illustrated chapter covers — confirmed

Every story chapter (5–20 story pages) includes one dedicated illustrated title cover at no additional credit charge. The cover is an additional asset: 5 story pages + cover = 6 images, billed 5 credits; 20 story pages + cover = 21 images, billed 20 credits. Individual quest comics have no separate cover by default.

Cover content uses the chapter title, relevant setting, chosen art style, and approved character reference. Historical gear must remain consistent with the chapter's depicted time. The cover is stored separately from billable comic_pages. The all-or-nothing policy includes the cover: the generation request cannot complete unless both cover and all story pages are ready; terminal failure releases the full credit reservation.
